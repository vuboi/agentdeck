### What's new

- **Sub-agents and background work stay under their own card.** A sub-agent's steps and words are drawn inside the tool call that started it (with its name, how many tools it ran and a running indicator) instead of mixing into the main answer. Claude, Codex, Copilot and OpenCode are mapped; any provider added later maps onto the same events.
- **Background jobs no longer lose their report.** When a sub-agent or a `run_in_background` shell ends, Claude (and Copilot) go on by themselves. The chat now shows that as a new reply of its own, and Stop works on it.
- **A common layer for providers:** each provider declares what it can do (mid-turn messages, parallel messages, turns of its own) and maps its output onto shared events, so new providers extend it instead of changing the chat. See `docs/development/adding-agent-provider.md`.

### Fixes

- **Chat jumbled when several sub-agents ran:** Claude's sub-agent lines were drawn as steps of the main reply, and Copilot's sub-agent text was appended to the answer. They are now scoped to their call.
- **Chat stuck after a background job:** the first result of a turn used to close it while the job ran, and what the CLI said when the job ended was never read. It is read now, and a job that never ends (a dev server) no longer holds the chat or trips the stall watchdog.
- **Copilot stop** reads until the session is idle instead of cutting the stream.
