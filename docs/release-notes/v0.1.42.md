### What's new

- **Smaller chat history.** Pictures you paste, drop or capture are stored as WebP, no wider or taller than 2048 px. Chat history and the Audit log load much faster. GIF and WebP pictures are kept as they are, and a picture that cannot be read is kept as it came.
- **Clear limit messages.** When a provider hits a limit, the composer names the kind of limit (five-hour, daily, weekly, monthly, request, credit or plan) and says what to do. Only limits with a known reset resume on their own. Credit and plan limits wait for you.
- **Stop all agents.** One action stops every running agent.
- **Replies in your language.** Lead models and delegated workers write replies and progress updates in the language you have been using, even when attached files or tool output are in another language.

### Fixes

- **Page freezes:** the preferences database runs off the UI thread, so saving settings and reading chat data no longer stalls the page.
- **Attachments:** pictures and files saved under your AgentDeck folder show as previews again.
- **Sidebar tree:** the tree is one shared component, so folding and Show more / Show less behave the same everywhere.
