### What's new

- **This is a fix release.** There are no new features.

### Fixes

- **Long reasoning in the step list folds properly:** reasoning over about 600 characters is shown short, with Show more and Show less buttons. Before, it sat in a small scrolling box.
- **No seam at the fold of a reasoning card:** the fade at the bottom now matches the card's colour, so the cut-off text no longer shows a band of a different background.
- **Messages to OpenCode are sent as typed:** a `&` or `|` in a message could start a second command on Windows, and very long pastes could hit the command-line length limit. The text now goes to OpenCode a different way, so neither happens.

### Install

Windows: `.exe` or `.msi`. macOS: `.dmg` (Apple silicon or Intel). Linux: `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
