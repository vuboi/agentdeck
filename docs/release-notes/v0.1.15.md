### What's new

- **Long pastes become a file:** pasting more than 10,000 characters into the message box adds them as a "Pasted text.txt" attachment instead of filling the prompt.
- **Panels slide:** the sidebar and the tools panel now slide open and closed. On a narrow window, the tools panel shrinks to fit instead of squeezing the chat.
- **Longer chat lists:** Show more in the sidebar loads 20 more chats at a time, and Recents is no longer cut off at 15.

### Fixes

- **Sidebar folders stay as you left them:** which folders are open is saved across restarts, and it is the same in the narrow-window drawer. Folding the sidebar no longer resets it.
- **Antigravity no longer shows as signed out right after you sign in:** AgentDeck now checks the saved sign-in itself instead of relying on Antigravity's logs, which could lag behind.
- **Antigravity usage shows at once:** Settings shows the last saved figures right away and keeps them if the live request fails. Before, the cards were blank until the request finished.
- **Clearer way to add an Antigravity account:** once you are signed in, a "Sign in another account" button explains how to add the next one.
- **Smoother sidebar with long lists:** opening or closing a large group no longer stutters.

### Install

Windows: `.exe` or `.msi`. macOS: `.dmg` (Apple silicon or Intel). Linux: `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
