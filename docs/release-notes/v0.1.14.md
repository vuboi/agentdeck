### What's new

- **Computer use (Windows):** turn it on in Settings > Computer use to let Claude, Codex and Antigravity take screenshots, click, type, press keys and scroll on your computer. Each action waits for you to approve it in the chat, and a request expires after 3 minutes.
- **Switch accounts from the chat:** the bar above the message box shows the account in use for Claude, Codex or Antigravity. You can switch to another saved account there, or sign in again when one has expired. Accounts that hit their limit are marked and cannot be picked.
- **Quota under the message box:** a small bar shows how much of the selected provider's limit is left, when it resets, and when the figure was last read.
- **Several Antigravity accounts:** save more than one Google account for Antigravity and switch between them in Settings. Sign-ins are kept in Windows Credential Manager. Quota is read live from Antigravity, with the last saved figures used when it can't be reached.
- **Codex free resets:** Settings shows how many free resets each Codex account has, as reported by ChatGPT, and lets you use one.
- **Tray menu:** the tray icon has New chat, your recent chats and Exit. Dev servers started by agents keep running until you choose Exit.
- **Image runs keep going in the background:** closing the image panel or switching tabs no longer stops a generation. It shows in Tasks while it runs and in the Audit log when it ends, and what you typed is still there when you come back. Prompt suggestions can also come from Antigravity or OpenCode, and more image models are offered.
- **Revert a change from the chat:** each file edit in a reply's step list has a Revert button. Diffs open at once, and long ones load 100 lines at a time.

### Fixes

- **Chats open where you left them:** coming back to a chat restores where you were reading, and a chat you left at the bottom opens at the bottom. Open step lists stay open.
- **A fork is a separate chat:** before, a fork showed the parent's replies again, ran in the parent's conversation and changed the parent's status.
- **Agent answers no longer wait for a busy chat:** when delegated agents finish while the chat is running another turn, their answers are handled right away in a turn alongside it.
- **Queued messages wait for your answer:** they are not sent while a question or permission request is open. The queue shows "waiting for your answer" and can always be cleared.
- **Picking an element no longer makes the page flash,** and screenshots from tools stay in the chat after a reload.
- **Settings opens without reloading every connector** each time. Refresh does that on demand, and its icon spins while it works.

### Install

Windows: `.exe` or `.msi`. macOS: `.dmg` (Apple silicon or Intel). Linux: `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
