### What's new

- **Long chats open fast and smooth:** history is read a page at a time, the newest messages first; older ones load as you scroll up, with a small indicator. Switching chats shows a skeleton and then the chat already at its end, without flashes.
- **Enter queues, Ctrl+Enter interrupts:** while the agent works. The queue has a Clear all button.
- **Sidebar folds away:** button in the header or Ctrl+B; headings, folders and "Show more" animate; the version is shown in the footer.
- **Finding the CLIs:** Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity are found in the usual install folders of each OS, in the PATH of your login shell on macOS and Linux, or from `AGENTDECK_<NAME>_BIN`.
- **Squarer corners:** two sizes (4px and 6px) for the whole app; popovers, tree rows and empty states tidied.
- **Usage:** project picker is a popover with search; empty states have icons.
- **Fixes:** `+` no longer makes a second "New session"; a closed chat is no longer highlighted; focus rings are off.

### Install

Windows: `.exe` or `.msi`. macOS: `.dmg` (Apple silicon or Intel). Linux: `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
