### What's new

- **A clearer cost chart in Usage:** the chart sits in its own frame, with a scale on the left (top, middle, zero) and guide lines. The heading names the busiest hour, day or week and what it cost. Point at a bar to see its time, its cost and what each agent spent; hours or days with nothing say so. A small cost is still drawn as a thin bar instead of vanishing.

### Fixes

- **The Workflow tab's empty text** no longer lists three agents by name: it says to pick a flow in the model menu or to type `@` and choose any agent or provider.

### Install

Windows: `.exe` or `.msi`. macOS: `.dmg` (Apple silicon or Intel). Linux: `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
